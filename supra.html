SUPRA Concept

A visual exploration of Georgian hospitality through architectural visualization and digital presentation. The SUPRA restaurant concept blends national motifs with a contemporary interior design language.

Visual Direction

Warm lighting, deep materials and handcrafted detailing define the space. Each element was considered to support an atmosphere of gathered, unhurried hospitality.
